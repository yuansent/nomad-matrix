# Nomad Matrix Relocation Reference Toolkit

This package contains general document examples and planning worksheets. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer or tax professional. It does not establish visa eligibility, consular acceptance, tax residency, insurance acceptance, or contract enforceability.

## Included files

- Employer remote work authorization example (.docx)
- Contractor agreement addendum example (.docx)
- Applicant cover letter and document index example (.docx)
- Travel day counter and simplified budget worksheet (.xlsx)

Confirm required wording, supporting documents, deadlines, and insurance coverage with the relevant authority. Only include statements that are true for your circumstances. Obtain local legal and tax advice where needed.

## Worksheet limitations

The workbook uses a simplified 183-day threshold and indicative country tax figures. Its existing status labels and formulas are planning aids, not determinations of tax residency or compliance. Being below 183 days does not establish tax exemption. Residency can depend on other tests, treaties, and counting periods. The tax and living-cost figures are not a live or professionally verified dataset.
